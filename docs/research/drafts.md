## Patterns across the strongest drafts (INFERRED)
- **Evidence and anti-hallucination guards** are now table stakes at the top: verbatim-quote checks, deterministic gates, confidence floors, "blank instead of invent". To stand out, a submission needs more than that.
- The strongest entries use **AssemblyAI-specific features by name**: streaming diarization with SpeakerRevision, keyterms, Voice Agent JSON-Schema tools, voice_focus, LLM Gateway, and multi-agent Voice Agent sessions.
- **No-microphone guided demos** (LoudEnough, Bisik) are becoming a norm for judge accessibility.
- Themes to avoid, as crowded: emergency triage, receptionist/front desk, restaurant ordering, voice RAG, interview/sales coach, incident commander.
- **Under-served combos**: human-to-human listening agents (like Bisik) and multi-party call control (like Awaaz).
